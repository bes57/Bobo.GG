THE ANSWER — IT MAKES MONEY, BUT ALMOST NONE OF IT IS THE MODEL. The rule returns +18.59% on the T−2h price and +23.47% on T−1h, over 253 matches, stable across both halves of the window. But backing every market underdog with BenPom switched off returns +17.13%. The rule is worth about +1.5 points on top of that. What is being harvested is the book's favourite–longshot bias — a 65c favourite wins only 58.5% — not a model insight.

1The rule

Take the YES price an hour or two before the match starts. Buy the side where BenPom differs from it by 5 percentage points or more. One contract. Hold to settlement. No fees.

The 5pp threshold is given, not fitted — so there is no swept maximum here, no train/test split to argue about, and the whole sample counts. Prices come from the raw candle book rebuilt against the real scheduled start, so nothing is sampled after a match began.

4What this does and does not establish

Does: over 253 matches from 2026-05-15 to 2026-08-12, at pre-match prices taken against the real scheduled start, the rule was profitable and stable across halves. The bias driving it is measurable and in the expected direction — the market's favourites are priced at 65c and win 58.5%.

Does not:

  • Show a model edge. +1.5 points over a no-model control, on a sample whose interval is roughly ±25 points, is not evidence that BenPom is beating the book. The honest description of this rule is "buy underdogs".
  • Include fees. None are charged, per the spec. For reference only: a taker paying Kalshi's ceil(0.07·C·P·(1−P)) would give back roughly six points on a 34.6c ticket. Maker fees on this series are genuinely zero.
  • Include adverse selection. The live week measured 8.5c per contract — you get filled preferentially when you are wrong. On a 34.6c ticket that is most of the margin, and no backtest that assumes you transact at the quoted price can see it. That week lost $309 over 719 fills.
  • Cover a full season. Three months, one title, top-tier VCT only.
Standing. Reporting only. Market data is never a fitting target or a selection signal, and nothing here changes the deployed model or the trading configuration.
