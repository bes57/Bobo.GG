1 The question, and what v6 does today

The production model's only roster mechanism is year-boundary continuity (carry-over weight 0.3 scaled by the January roster overlap). Every mid-season change — ENVY's three 2026 swaps included — is invisible to the solve: the team keeps its full rating as if nothing happened. This report measures what that costs, forensically and population-wide, and takes three preregistered exploratory shots at fixing it.

Change definition (preregistered before computing): a rotation-guarded lineup-set change vs the previous match; sustained = the new five holds ≥3 consecutive matches (or is censored by the data edge); magnitude = lineup overlap |A∩B|/max(|A|,|B|,5) — the engine's own formula. 7 Bot integration + the prospective plan

Tier 1 — sizing only RECOMMENDED NOW

build_model_snapshot adds a per-team roster_flag block; the bot sizes down / widens quotes on fresh rosters. Zero fair-value risk: the model's probabilities are untouched.

  • matches_since_change <= 2 AND overlap_vs_prev <= 0.6 -> quarter-size, widen quote by one tick
  • matches_since_change <= 2 AND overlap_vs_prev == 0.8 -> half-size
  • matches_since_change >= 3 -> normal sizing

Basis: the atlas's elevated, directional post-change error and the case evidence (ENVY +24.3pp, LEV -14.7pp per match). Sizing down on elevated-uncertainty rows needs no directional model claim and zero fair-value risk. Snapshot fields + data path in the JSON.

Tier 2 — fair-value change NOT RECOMMENDED NOW

all three exploratory reads were negative on the (spent) holdout: (b) -3.889m (falsifier fired), (c) -1.423m (inside the 1.77m floor, no support), (d) phase-reset -19.275m vs v6 and -7.524m vs its own filter base (falsifier fired; contrast prediction reversed). v6's year-boundary continuity 0.3 remains the champion roster mechanism.

Player-identity mean shift (design note only)

at a change event, shift the reference-point mean by a walk-forward estimate of (incoming - outgoing) player quality (e.g. decayed per-map rating share or PTD-style contribution from data/enriched/player_map_advanced.csv), scaled by (1 - ov). Directional where the phase-reset was agnostic: Neon-in would shift LEV UP, inspire-out ENVY DOWN. No holdout read was spent on it (operator addendum).
